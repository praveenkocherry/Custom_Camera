Custom Camera

Custom Camera is a free camera app that runs in your phone's browser and can be installed like an app. It takes photos and videos, scans documents into PDFs, reads QR codes, turns pictures into text labels, and can read the world aloud for people with low vision.

Everything you capture is saved straight to your own Google Drive, in a folder called "Custom Camera". The app can only see files it created itself.

Open Custom Camera

How it uses Google Drive

You sign in with your Google account and allow one permission, "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). The app uses it to save, list, favourite, restore and delete the files it created. See the Privacy Policy for details and the Terms of Service.
