Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. This policy covers the Custom Camera web app at https://praveenkocherry.github.io/Custom_Camera/ (the "app").

What the app accesses

  • Camera, microphone and motion sensors: used only while you use the app, to take photos, record video with sound, scan, read and detect phone tilt. Nothing is streamed to us.
  • Google Drive (drive.file permission): the app creates a folder called "Custom Camera" and saves your photos, videos, scans and text labels there. It can only see, change or delete files that it created. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

How your data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you see: saving, showing, favouriting, sharing, restoring and deleting your own captures. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone. The app has no server and no analytics; we do not receive, store or see your files or your Google data.

Where data is stored

  • Your captures are stored in your own Google Drive.
  • Small thumbnails, a list of recent items, captures waiting to upload, QR history, settings and the Google access token are kept in your browser's own storage on your device. Clearing the browser data removes them.

Optional AI features

Text and Read modes can send a picture, audio or video frame to Google's Gemini service, but only if you add your own Gemini API key in the app. The key is stored only on your device and used directly between your device and Google, under Google's own terms. Without a key, these features are off.

Sharing

We share nothing. Files leave your device only to your Google Drive, to Google Gemini if you enabled it, or when you choose Share and pick an app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can delete any file from within the app or from Google Drive. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
